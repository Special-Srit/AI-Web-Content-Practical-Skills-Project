### Commonly repeated but wrong or stale (2026-08)

- “Melon is Korea’s undisputed #1 music app in 2026.”  
  Correct: YouTube Music ranks #1 in both publicly reported June-2026 datasets. WiseApp places Spotify above Melon; Mobile Index places Melon above Spotify. [Korea Economic Daily comparison](https://www.hankyung.com/article/202607280789i).

- “YouTube Music has 125m paid subscribers.”  
  Correct: YouTube reported 125m+ combined YouTube Music and YouTube Premium subscribers, including trials, as of 2025-03-05. It is not a standalone YouTube Music paid-subscriber count. [YouTube](https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/20-years-125-million-subscribers-lyor-cohen/).

- “Spotify has 761m paid subscribers.”  
  Correct: Spotify reported 761m MAU and 293m paid Premium subscribers as of 2026-03-31. [Spotify SEC filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639920/000162828026027951/spot-20260331x6k.htm).

- “Apple Music has 100m subscribers.”  
  Correct: Apple’s current public claim is 100m+ songs; it does not disclose a current Apple Music subscriber or MAU figure. [Apple Music](https://www.apple.com/apple-music/), [Apple Services announcement](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/01/2025-marked-a-record-breaking-year-for-apple-services/).

- “Amazon Music has 100m subscribers.”  
  Correct: 100m+ refers to songs in the catalog, not subscribers. Current Amazon Music subscriber/MAU totals are not publicly disclosed. [Amazon Music](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/entertainment/amazon-music-price).

- “Spotify Free is always shuffle-only with six skips per hour.”  
  Correct: Spotify’s current controls vary by market/account, and Spotify changed Free playback controls in 2025. The universal six-skips claim is stale/unverified. [Spotify shuffle update](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-11-13/shuffle-update-fewer-repeats/).

- “YouTube Music Free is shuffle-only and cannot play songs on demand.”  
  Correct: Current YouTube Music Help documents ad-supported on-demand playback and unlimited skips; Premium is required for music background playback and downloads. [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtubemusic/answer/6313529?hl=en).

- “YouTube Premium Lite includes YouTube Music.”  
  Correct: Korea’s Premium Lite launch price was ₩8,500/month as of 2026-01-30, and Google explicitly says Premium Lite does not include YouTube Music Premium. [YouTube Korea](https://blog.youtube/intl/ko-kr/news-and-events/yt-premium-lite-kr/), [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15968883?hl=en-EN).

- “FLO shut down or disappeared after ownership changes.”  
  Correct: FLO remained active and was described in a 2026 Q1 filing; bemyfriends announced acquisition of Dreamus, FLO’s operator, in 2025. [FLO filing](https://kind.krx.co.kr/external/2026/05/14/000355/20260514000754/11013.htm), [bemyfriends announcement](https://bemyfriends.com/article/bemyfriends-dreamus-company-acquisition-en/?hl=en).

- “FLO has exactly 100m songs.”  
  Correct: The latest company filing claims more than 130m songs as of 2026-03-31, while the App Store listing still says 100m. The figure is company-claimed and internally inconsistent. [FLO filing](https://kind.krx.co.kr/external/2026/05/14/000355/20260514000754/11013.htm), [FLO App Store](https://apps.apple.com/kr/app/flo-플로/id1129048043).

- “Kakao Music is a separate current top Korean streaming service.”  
  Correct: Kakao’s music-streaming business is centered on Melon; Kakao Entertainment merged with Melon in 2021. [Kakao Entertainment](https://newsroom.kakaoent.com/news/kakao-entertainment-merges-with-koreas-no-1-music-streaming-platform-melon-as-it-eyes-global-growth/).

- “YouTube Premium Korea costs ₩10,450” or “Spotify Korea Individual costs ₩10,900.”  
  Correct current listed prices are ₩14,900 for YouTube Premium and ₩11,990 for Spotify Korea Individual, checked 2026-08-04. [YouTube Korea](https://blog.youtube/intl/ko-kr/news-and-events/yt-premium-lite-kr/), [Spotify Korea](https://www.spotify.com/kr-ko/premium/?nd=1).

### Unverified or unresolved

- Korea has no single authoritative national MAU ranking. WiseApp and IGAWorks Mobile Index produce materially different June-2026 results. WiseApp is newer in the accessible report; Mobile Index has a direct official publication and a longer published series. Both remain estimates rather than audited subscriber data.

- YouTube Music’s standalone global paid subscribers and MAU are not disclosed. The 125m figure combines Music and Premium and includes trials.

- Apple Music and Amazon Music do not currently disclose comparable global subscriber or MAU figures.

- NetEase Cloud Music’s FY2025 release reports growth but not an exact current MAU or paying-user count.

- Current total catalog sizes for Melon, Genie Music and Bugs could not be verified from accessible official sources.

- FLO’s official catalog claims conflict: over 130m in a 2026 filing versus 100m in its App Store listing.

- Spotify Free’s exact skip, shuffle and audio-quality rules are account- and market-dependent; the old universal “six skips per hour” statement should not be used without specifying the tested market and account state.

- Korea-specific YouTube student and family pricing was not publicly verifiable. The Korean launch page exposes individual web/Android prices only.
