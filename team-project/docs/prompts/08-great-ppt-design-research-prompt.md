## Questions to answer

1. **Vertical rhythm / whitespace usage** — When a slide's content (a short
   table, a few bullet points) doesn't fill the available content area, what do
   professional slide designers actually do? Options to investigate: vertically
   centering the content block instead of top-anchoring it, increasing type
   size/line-height to fill space intentionally, adding a secondary visual
   element (stat, quote, image) to balance the layout, or deliberately
   accepting whitespace as a design choice (and when that reads as
   "intentional minimalism" vs. "empty/unfinished"). Cite real sources (Duarte,
   Presentation Zen/Garr Reynolds, Nancy Duarte's "Slide:ology" principles
   summarized in interviews/articles, McKinsey-style slide design guides,
   SlideModel/Envato/Canva design-principle articles, or academic
   typography/layout sources) — not just your own opinion.
2. **Typographic scale and hierarchy** — Is there a standard ratio or system
   for how much bigger a title should be than a subtitle, and subtitle than
   body text, in a 16:9 slide? (E.g. type-scale ratios like 1.25/1.333/1.5,
   or specific pt recommendations for 16:9 decks.)
3. **Grid systems and alignment** — What's the standard practice for a
   consistent content grid across all slides in a deck (margins, safe content
   area, consistent left/top anchors)? How do designers keep a deck feeling
   like "one system" across content-heavy slides, a divider/statement slide,
   and a flow-diagram slide?
4. **Color usage ratio** — Is there a standard guideline for how much of a
   slide's area a single strong accent color should occupy (e.g. the 60-30-10
   rule, or similar), and what happens visually when an accent color is used
   inconsistently across a deck (heavy fill on one slide, thin accent lines on
   another)?
5. **Decorative shape placement rules** — What's the standard guidance for
   placing a decorative/background shape (e.g. a large geometric accent) so it
   doesn't collide with foreground text — safe zones, opacity/layering rules,
   or z-order conventions?
6. **Table/data-slide design specifically** — For a slide whose content is
   mainly a short comparison table (2-3 rows, not filling the vertical space),
   what do professional designers do to keep it feeling complete rather than
   sparse? Column-width balancing conventions?
7. **Korean-specific typography notes** — Any real, sourced guidance on line
   height, tracking, or type size specifically for Korean (Hangul) body text in
   presentation contexts, since Latin-typography rules of thumb don't always
   transfer directly.
